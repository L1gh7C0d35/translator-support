TRANSLATE · HELP

How can we help?

Camera translation

Allow Camera permission when requested. Point the camera at a sign, label or menu, keep it steady and use the shutter to capture a clearer document. Translated text may need extra room. Always check quantities, prices, instructions and small print against the original.

Voice and conversations

Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition when prompted. Some spoken-language recognition may need an internet connection or downloaded speech resources. Avoid relying on automatic translation for medical, legal, emergency or safety-critical communication.

Offline languages

On iOS 17, Translate uses Google ML Kit's on-device translation. On iOS 18 and later, you can also use Apple's supported on-device languages. For the Extended engine, connect to Wi-Fi to download the models for your chosen languages before traveling. Model availability and quality vary by language.

Free translation and optional Pro

Text translation, Camera text recognition, manual two-way Talk, mirrored Face-to-Face, Live Captions, saved phrases and supported offline language preparation remain available without a subscription or Translate account. Pro is optional and adds document translation, detailed menu review, webpage text translation, automatic alternating conversation turns, travel boards, personal glossaries and speaking practice. Choose Settings → Membership → Explore Pro to see the actual monthly and annual plan prices, renewal terms and any introductory offers you are eligible for. Apple handles billing. To recover an existing verified subscription, choose Restore purchases in Settings; subscription cancellation and management happen through your Apple Account. TestFlight Pro preview controls, where present, do not purchase or restore an Apple subscription.

Privacy and deletion

Saved translations, captions and any previously saved travel information stay in the app's local storage. Open Settings in Translate to delete saved translations, captions or travel extras. Deleting the app removes its local container, subject to device backup and restore settings. Read the privacy policy for information about Google ML Kit diagnostics and Apple speech services.

Contact support

Email beenken@gmail.com or open an issue on GitHub. Please include iPhone/iPad model, iOS version and app build number. Do not publicly post private documents, transcripts, payment information or personally identifying images.
