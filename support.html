TRANSLATE · HELP

How can we help?

Camera translation

Allow Camera permission when requested. Point the camera at a sign, label or menu, keep it steady and use the shutter to capture a clearer document. Translated text may need extra room. Always check quantities, prices, instructions and small print against the original.

Voice and conversations

Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition when prompted. Some spoken-language recognition may need an internet connection or downloaded speech resources. Avoid relying on automatic translation for medical, legal, emergency or safety-critical communication.

Offline languages

On iOS 17, Translate uses Google ML Kit's on-device translation. On iOS 18 and later, you can also use Apple's supported on-device languages. For the Extended engine, connect to Wi-Fi to download the models for your chosen languages before traveling. Model availability and quality vary by language.

Pro and purchases

Text, camera and voice translation are included in the free app. Advanced document, travel and productivity tools may require Pro. If purchased through Apple, choose Restore Purchases inside the app after signing in with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple Subscriptions.

Privacy and deletion

Saved translations and travel information are kept in the app's device storage. Open Settings in Translate to clear saved phrases or travel data. Deleting the app removes its local app container, subject to your device backup settings. Read the privacy policy for information about Google ML Kit diagnostics and Apple speech services.

Contact support

Email beenken@gmail.com or open an issue on GitHub. Please include iPhone/iPad model, iOS version and app build number. Do not publicly post private documents, transcripts, payment information or personally identifying images.
