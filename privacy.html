PRIVACY POLICY

Your words stay yours.

This policy describes data handling in Translate: Camera & Voice, an iPhone and iPad app developed by L1gh7C0d35. Effective October 13, 2026.

Translation and local data

Text, photographed words and translated results are processed by supported on-device Apple and Google ML Kit translation technologies where available. Camera text recognition uses Apple Vision. The developer does not operate a server that receives your translated text, captured photos or saved documents. Saved phrases, favorites, glossary items, travel boards, and conversation archives are stored in the app's local device container and may be included in iPhone or iPad backups according to your system settings.

Speech recognition uses Apple's Speech services and may require network processing for certain languages or devices; Apple's privacy practices apply to those services. We do not operate a separate speech-upload service.

Google ML Kit third-party diagnostics

The app includes Google ML Kit translation and language identification. Google documents that ML Kit may collect device and app details, per-installation identifiers, performance and usage metrics, API configuration, source/destination or detected language codes, and Firebase-related installation/remote-configuration information for diagnostics and analytics. This is separate from the words or photos being translated, which ML Kit's on-device translation handles locally. Google may process diagnostic information under its own policies.

See Google's ML Kit data disclosure, Google's Privacy Policy and ML Kit usage guidelines.

Permissions

Camera permission enables live text scanning; Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions enable spoken phrases and conversations; selecting a photo accesses only the items you choose through iOS controls. You can change app permissions at any time in Settings on your device. The app does not require a Translate account and does not run third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Purchases

If you buy Pro, Apple manages billing, subscription renewal, payment details and refunds. Translate checks verified StoreKit purchase entitlements to provide access; it does not collect or store your credit card number. You can manage subscriptions through your Apple Account and use Restore Purchases inside the app.

Your choices and retention

Use Settings in the app to delete stored translations and travel data. You can revoke Camera, Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions through iOS Settings, choose available translation engines and manage offline model downloads. Local records remain until removed, the app is uninstalled or device backups are restored. Diagnostics collected independently by third-party SDKs may have their own retention periods; contact those providers for their policies.

Contact and policy changes

For privacy questions or deletion support, email beenken@gmail.com. Material policy changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

Automatically translated content may be inaccurate. Don't rely on this app as a substitute for a qualified interpreter for safety-critical, legal, medical or financial matters.
